What’s Included in Your LTx Crate Engine

Every Golen LTx engine is hand-built using premium American-made components and tailored to your exact needs.


Included Features:

  • Custom Assembled Long Block: Machined LTx heads, forged internals, blueprinted for reliability
  • Intake System: Complete with throttle body, fuel rails, and injectors
  • Optional Ignition Components: Coils, spark plugs, and wiring available
  • 1-Year Unlimited Mileage Warranty: Confidence backed by craftsmanship


Boost-Ready and Built to Run Hard

Thinking of going blown? Every LTx crate engine is engineered for forced induction. Whether you're adding a centrifugal supercharger, a Roots blower, or a turbo setup, our internal components are built to handle big boost and extreme RPM.


Want naturally aspirated performance? We’ve got you covered there, too—with clean, reliable horsepower and torque.


Engineered In-House. Dyno-Verified Performance.

At Golen Engine, we perform all machining, blueprinting, balancing, and assembly in our New Hampshire facility. Your engine is:

  • Fully balanced and blueprinted
  • Broken in with a dual oil change cycle
  • Dyno-tested with a printed horsepower/torque graph included

We deliver proven performance, not guesses.
