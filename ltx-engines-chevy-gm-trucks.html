What’s Included in Every LTx Crate Engine

Each Golen crate motor is hand-assembled with top-tier American components, including:

  • Custom-machined LTx Long Block: High-flow CNC heads, forged internals, and balanced rotating assembly
  • Complete Intake System: Includes throttle body, fuel rails, and injectors
  • Optional Ignition Add-ons: Coils, plugs, and wire sets
  • Boost-Ready Internals: Optimized for superchargers and turbo builds

Every engine is dyno-tested and comes with a 1-year, unlimited mileage warranty.
