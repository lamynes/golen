Upgrade your LS engine with Golen Engine’s CNC-ported LS cylinder heads, engineered for maximum performance in both naturally aspirated and boosted builds. Available in 4-bolt and 6-bolt configurations, these heads are ideal for LS1 and LS3 platforms and are precision-crafted to deliver outstanding airflow and power gains.


Precision Milled LS Cylinder Heads

Every Golen LS head undergoes 5-axis CNC porting for unmatched accuracy and flow consistency. After porting, each head is hand-blended for smooth transitions, receives a 3-angle competition valve job, and is milled to your desired deck height for optimal compression. Final assembly is tailored to match your camshaft profile, ensuring top-end reliability and efficiency.


Key Components Include:

  • LS3 Cylinder Heads: REV stainless steel 2.165" intake / 1.600" exhaust valves
  • LS1 Cylinder Heads: REV stainless steel 2.020" intake / 1.600" exhaust valves
  • Valvetrain: PAC Racing springs with titanium retainers
  • Billet Rocker Arm Pedestals for added durability under extreme RPMs


These LS CNC cylinder heads are flow-tested, cleaned, and fully assembled in-house to the highest standards. Whether you're chasing track times or street dominance, Golen delivers proven performance backed by decades of LS engine expertise.
